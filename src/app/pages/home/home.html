About the Application

The Enquiry Management System helps businesses handle customer queries efficiently. It allows staff to record, update, and track enquiries, ensuring no request is missed.

How It Works

1. Receive Enquiry

Customer submits enquiry.

2. Assign Staff

Enquiry assigned to staff member.

3. Process

Staff responds and updates status.

4. Close

Enquiry marked as closed.
