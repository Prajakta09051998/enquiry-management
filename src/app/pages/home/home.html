About the Application

The Enquiry Management System helps businesses handle customer queries efficiently. It allows staff to record, update, and track enquiries, ensuring no request is missed. The system centralizes all customer enquiries in one place, making it easier for teams to manage communication and maintain organized records. Each enquiry can include important details such as the customer’s name, contact information, the nature of the request, and the date of submission. This ensures that staff members have all the necessary information readily available when responding to customers.

How It Works

1. Receive Enquiry

Customer submits enquiry.

2. Assign Staff

Enquiry assigned to staff member.

3. Process

Staff responds and updates status.

4. Close

Enquiry marked as closed.
